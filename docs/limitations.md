# Limitations

What this version does not do:

- A chamfered or rounded top edge of the plate.
- Engraved (sunken) images, lithophanes.
- Elements on the back face, except hidden keyholes.
- Vector fonts from font files, or uploading a font: texts are drawn with
  the bundled fonts and traced.
- Multi-part STL or 3MF for multi-material printers: one STL and a
  colour-change hint.
- Magnets, stands, mounting accessories; curved or bent plates.

Limits: plates 10–300 mm, thickness 0.6–20 mm; up to 8 images (512 px
kept, files up to 20 MB), 10 texts of 10 lines and 300 characters, 16 holes;
reliefs up to 10 mm above the base.

On a 300 mm plate with a picture across it, the preview takes about two
seconds and the export about fifteen.
